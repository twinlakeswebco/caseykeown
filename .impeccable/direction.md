# Hometown magazine
Approved by user through structured question. Code-first static HTML/CSS.

A personal local-magazine profile: warm ivory paper, deep blue condensed Barlow headlines, restrained red punctuation, a large real portrait, and an editorial archive with publisher/date metadata. Not the restaurant's menu or booking page. First viewport introduces Casey personally and opens Facebook contact. Route: profile → biography → interview → press → photos → connect. Photos and press retain source attribution. Mobile stacks the portrait and introduction and turns press metadata into horizontal rows. Keep visual headings at or below 96px; no generated identity imagery.
